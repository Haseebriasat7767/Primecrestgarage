Our story

The Best Car Service Center in Dubai

A luxury car deserves more than a generic workshop. Primecrest Garage was built as a lounge for machines — a climate-controlled, granite-floored facility in Al Quoz where premium vehicles are the only vehicles we touch.

Our technicians specialise in advanced diagnostics, engine repair, braking systems, wheel alignment, suspension rebuilds and fuel-system service. Every instruction is measured, photographed and recorded, then explained to you in plain language before a single tool is picked up.
